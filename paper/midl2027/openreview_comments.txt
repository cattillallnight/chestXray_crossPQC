This manuscript isolates what a six-qubit head can and cannot change, relative to a classical head, on a shared six-dimensional bottleneck from a frozen DenseNet-121. It does not propose a new architecture. A key methodological point is stated openly: with a single-qubit readout <Z0> and no entangling gates, the product PQC is functionally a one-feature model (only z0 reaches the measured wire), so nominal parameter parity (6,188 vs 6,215) does not imply functional parity. The clean classical-versus-quantum comparison is therefore MLP versus entangling PQC, and we present it that way. Primary findings share a name on CheXpert and VinDr-CXR; pneumonia is appendix-only because VinDr has no Pneumonia class. Forty-five of forty-five runs are reported, including seven degenerate temperature fits; calibration is reported on both balanced and natural-prevalence VinDr holdouts; AUROC, AUPRC, F1, Brier, ECE and MCE are all reported. We do not claim quantum advantage, hardware execution, SOTA CXR detection, or clinical deployment.

Keywords: confidence calibration, domain shift, chest radiography, parameterized quantum circuit, matched capacity, readout locality
