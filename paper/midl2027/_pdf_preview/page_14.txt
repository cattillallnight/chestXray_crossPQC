Appendix D. Reproducibility
Interpreter: Python 3.10, PyTorch 2.6.0+cu124, PennyLane 0.38.0. Config: configs/default.yaml.
Grid: scripts/run queue.py (45/45 complete). Tables: results/tables/all runs.csv.
Checkpoints: results/runs/. Splits were not resampled after the grid. Logits for reliabil-
ity diagrams are dumped from checkpoints by scripts/dump logits.py. Training code,
frozen split files, and run JSON will be released with the camera-ready version if the paper
is accepted; they are not on a public repository at submission. CheXpert and VinDr-CXR
are public de-identified datasets. The author has no competing interests.
