4.2. Entanglement is not a calibrator
The primary question is calibration, not ranking AUROC. Entangling circuits do not yield
lower source ECE (Table 3; Figure 3). Where a PQC is better calibrated on CheXpert, it is
the product circuit—no finding and pleural effusion—not the CNOT ring. The entangling
head on no finding seed 42 is the clearest single-run illustration: it over-predicts the positive
class (accuracy 0.560, sensitivity 0.951, ECE 0.419; Figure 5). Ring CNOTs at two layers
and a single-qubit readout increase expressibility in the sense of Sim et al. (Sim et al., 2019);
they do not give the optimizer a calibration incentive. Across the 12 primary pairs, product
ECE is lower than MLP ECE on 8 CheXpert tests; entangling ECE is lower on only 3.
On VinDr balanced data, raw ECE is sometimes lower for PQC heads, but that reduction
coexists with weaker or comparable AUROC and does not survive temperature transfer.
4.3. Source-fitted temperature need not travel
Temperature fitted on CheXpert validation is not a reliable target calibrator (Figure 4).
The intended use of Guo-style scaling is to correct overconfidence on a held-out slice of the
same corpus. CheXpert validation and test are natural-prevalence slices of that US corpus;
VinDr balanced evaluation forces prevalence 0.5 (or 0.353 for consolidation). A scalar T
cannot undo a prevalence and device shift (Ovadia et al., 2019; Guo et al., 2017). On no
finding product PQC, mean CheXpert ECE falls after scaling while VinDr balanced ECE
rises—the same T helps the source and harms the target. Seven of 45 runs hit the clamp
T ∈{0.001, 100} (Table 6): cardiomegaly entangling seed 43 (T = 100); consolidation MLP
seed 43 (T = 100); consolidation product seeds 42 (T = 0.001) and 44 (T = 100); and three
pneumonia-proxy runs in the appendix. Those fits flatten or sharpen probabilities until ECE
on one domain is a box-constraint artifact. They remain in Table 3. Removing them does
not reverse the no-finding or pleural-effusion rankings, where ndeg = 0. The cardiomegaly
entangling VinDr-balanced calibrated ECE of 0.039 ± 0.064 is dominated by the T = 100
run (calibrated ECE ≈0) and is not evidence of successful transfer. Source-fitted T does
not systematically help PQC more than MLP on VinDr-CXR.
