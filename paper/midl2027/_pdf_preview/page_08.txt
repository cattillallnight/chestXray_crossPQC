Figure 3: Grouped bars (mean over three seeds; error bars = std) for MLP, product PQC,
and entangling PQC. Pneumonia proxy is excluded.
Bounded ⟨Z0⟩∈[−1, 1] plus a learned scale and bias is a different hypothesis class from
an unconstrained MLP; it is not automatically better, and it is not entanglement. Second,
adding a CNOT ring at two layers with a single-qubit observable increases expressibility
without giving the optimizer a calibration incentive. On no finding seed 42 the entangling
head becomes a high-sensitivity, low-accuracy over-predictor.
Temperature scaling fails for a structural reason that does not require a quantum story.
A scalar T fitted to source negative log-likelihood cannot undo a prevalence and device
shift. When T hits 0.001 or 100, ECE on one domain becomes a box-constraint artifact.
Reporting those runs, rather than dropping them, is part of the result.
