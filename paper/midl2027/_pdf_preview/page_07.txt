5. Discussion
The protocol answers a smaller question than hybrid-imaging abstracts usually ask, and the
answer is negative. A six-qubit expectation head is not a more trustworthy replacement
for a 65-parameter MLP sitting on the same six angles. That is not a statement about
quantum machine learning in general. It is a statement about this ansatz, this readout, this
frozen trunk, this sample size, and this shift.
Two inductive-bias observations follow. First, the product circuit—not the entangling
circuit—is the PQC that occasionally lowers source ECE (no finding, pleural effusion).
