2.3. Parameterized quantum circuits
Parameterized quantum circuits are variational models whose gates carry trainable angles
(Mitarai et al., 2018; Benedetti et al., 2019; Cerezo et al., 2021). Angle embedding followed
by single-qubit rotations and optional entangling gates is the default encoding in PennyLane
(Bergholm et al., 2018). Schuld et al. (Schuld et al., 2021) showed that the encoding controls
the Fourier spectrum of the hypothesis class. Expressibility and entangling capability of
common ansatze were quantified by Sim et al. (Sim et al., 2019); those measures do not
imply better calibration. Training is further constrained by barren plateaus (McClean et al.,
2018). Hybrid transfer learning has already replaced a DenseNet-121 classifier with a PQC
for CheXpert cardiomegaly and reported AUROC comparable to a classical head (Decoodt
et al., 2023). That study optimizes discrimination on a single balanced corpus. It does not
match head capacity on a shared bottleneck or treat calibration under geographic shift as
the primary endpoint. A six-qubit expectation head on a frozen DenseNet vector is a small
classical-to-quantum map. Comparing it with a fully trained DenseNet classifier confounds
representation learning with the head.
Comparing a product circuit with an otherwise
identical CNOT-ring circuit does not.
3. Methods
3.1. Source, target, and tasks
CheXpert-v1.0-small is the source (Irvin et al., 2019). Only frontal radiographs are kept.
Uncertain labels (−1) are dropped; blank findings are treated as negative. Splits are patient-
level.
Training uses 2 000 class-balanced images; validation and test use 500 images at
natural prevalence (Table 1).
VinDr-CXR training images (15 000 PNG files) are the external target (Nguyen et al.,
2022). No VinDr image is used for training. An image is positive if any radiologist assigned
that class. Two holdouts of 1 000 images are built: class-balanced and natural-prevalence.
Consolidation has only 353 positives in the public training boxes, so the “balanced” holdout
is 353/647. The 3 000-image Kaggle test split has no public labels and is unused. Splits
were not resampled after the grid.
Four primary tasks share a finding name: no finding, cardiomegaly, pleural effusion,
and consolidation. A fifth task, pneumonia proxy, maps CheXpert Pneumonia to VinDr
Consolidation ∪Infiltration and is flagged ontology shift=True. It is not a pneumonia
detector.
