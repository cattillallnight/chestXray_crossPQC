6. Limitations
The circuit has six qubits, two layers, and a single observable. The backbone is frozen, so
the PQC never sees pixels. Simulation uses default.qubit, not hardware noise. Sample
sizes are small (train 2 000; val/test 500; VinDr 1 000).
Three seeds cannot support a
confirmatory hypothesis test. Temperature is a scalar with a hard clamp. ECE uses 15
equal-width bins; rare positives make several bins empty (Nixon et al., 2019). VinDr any-
vote ignores radiologist disagreement. ImageNet-normalized 224 × 224 RGB conversion is
a crude CXR preprocessing. No clinical deployment is claimed.
7. Conclusion
Under the protocol actually run—frozen ImageNet DenseNet-121, shared z ∈R6, six-qubit
two-layer product and ring-CNOT heads, noiseless default.qubit, three seeds—neither
PQC head is a more trustworthy replacement for the matched MLP on CheXpert →VinDr-
CXR. Discrimination is comparable or slightly worse. Entanglement does not reduce ECE.
Source-fitted temperature scaling can harm the target. Those are empirical boundaries for
this capacity and shift, not evidence against quantum machine learning in general.
