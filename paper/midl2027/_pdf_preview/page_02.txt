The paper does not claim a new ansatz, quantum advantage, hardware execution,
or state-of-the-art CXR detection. What the implementation can support is a matched-
capacity protocol, a fully reported negative grid, and a shift result for post-hoc calibration.
All heads consume the same z ∈R6. Product and entangling circuits differ only by a CNOT
ring, which adds no parameters (6 188 versus 6 215). The bottleneck is not detached, so
gradients into W are part of every comparison. A fully trained DenseNet classifier is an
unmatched upper bound and is not used as a baseline. Forty-five of forty-five scheduled
runs are included, among them seven temperature fits that hit the clamp T ∈{0.001, 100}.
CheXpert uncertain labels are dropped (U-Ignore) (Irvin et al., 2019). VinDr boxes use an
any-vote (Nguyen et al., 2022). Findings that share a name are reported in the main tables;
pneumonia is not, because VinDr-CXR has no Pneumonia class.
2. Related work
2.1. Confidence calibration
A probabilistic classifier is calibrated when, among instances predicted with confidence p,
the empirical frequency of the predicted event is also p. Brier (Brier, 1950) introduced
a proper scoring rule that jointly penalizes discrimination and calibration. Naeini et al.
(Naeini et al., 2015) defined ECE by binning confidence and averaging the absolute gap
between accuracy and confidence; Nixon et al. (Nixon et al., 2019) later documented the
sensitivity of that estimator to binning. Guo et al. (Guo et al., 2017) showed that modern
deep networks are systematically overconfident and that a single temperature T fitted by
negative log-likelihood on a validation set is a strong, almost parameter-free baseline. None
of these methods assumes that the validation distribution matches the deployment distri-
bution. Ovadia et al. (Ovadia et al., 2019) showed that predictive uncertainty, including
temperature-scaled confidence, degrades under covariate and semantic shift. We use Guo-
style scalar temperature as the third research question, fitted only on CheXpert validation
logits.
2.2. Chest radiography, labels, and shift
ChestX-ray8 (Wang et al., 2017), CheXpert (Irvin et al., 2019), MIMIC-CXR (Johnson
et al., 2019), and VinDr-CXR (Nguyen et al., 2022) differ in geography, devices, report
style, and label generation. CheXNet (Rajpurkar et al., 2017) reported radiologist-level
pneumonia detection on a single corpus; subsequent analyses showed that such numbers
need not travel.
