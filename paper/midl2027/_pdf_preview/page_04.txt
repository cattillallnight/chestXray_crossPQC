3.2. Matched-capacity heads
The model is a frozen trunk plus a small head. Let x ∈R1024 be the frozen DenseNet-121
feature. Images are converted to RGB, resized to 224 × 224, and ImageNet-normalized; the
backbone classifier is replaced by the identity. The bottleneck is
z = π tanh(Wx + b) ∈R6.
(1)
The MLP head is 6 →8 →tanh →Dropout(0.1) →1 (6 215 trainable parameters, including
W, b). Each PQC uses six wires, two layers of RX/RY/RZ, and Y -angle embedding of z.
The product circuit has no CNOTs; the entangling circuit adds a ring CNOT(i, i + 1) plus
CNOT(5, 0) after each rotation layer. The readout is scale · ⟨Z0⟩+ bias (6 188 parameters).
The bottleneck tensor is not detached: gradients flow into W for every head. Circuits are
simulated with PennyLane default.qubit and backpropagation on CPU float64. There
is no hardware run. Figure 1 is the shared trunk; Figure 2 is the circuit that models.py
actually builds.
